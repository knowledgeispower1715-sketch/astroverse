# AstroVerse Prediction Methodology & Parashari Jyotish Framework

## 1. Classical Astrological Foundation

AstroVerse prediction algorithms are derived from classical Vedic texts—chiefly the *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra* (BPHS), *Phaladeepika*, and the *Saravali*—combined with the classical Chaldean planetary order for diurnal and nocturnal Horas, Jaimini Upadesha Sutras, Krishnamurti Padhdhati (KP), and Tajika Neelakanthi for annual solar returns.

The platform rejects arbitrary superstition and fatalistic pronouncements. Instead, predictions represent qualitative astrological tendencies and auspicious time windows designed to empower informed, ethical action.

---

## 2. Standardized 9-Section Architecture

Every structured prediction generated across any horizon strictly complies with the standardized 9-section framework:

### Section 1: Executive Summary (`executive_summary`)
- **Purpose**: Provides a concise executive summary of celestial conditions for the selected timeframe.
- **Formulation**: Distills the core environmental and tactical message in 1–2 sentences.

### Section 2: Overall Outlook (`overall_outlook`)
- **Purpose**: Detailed synthesized outlook for the timeframe.
- **Formulation**: Synthesizes the active planetary ruler with the natal Lagna, transiting Moon, and prevailing Dasha cycle.

### Section 3: Potentially Supportive Themes (`supportive_themes`)
- **Purpose**: Identifies favorable endeavors, strategic opportunities, and harmonious alignments.
- **Formulation**: Evaluates benefic aspects (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, waxing Moon), Kendra/Trikona activations, and Dhana/Raja Yoga triggers.

### Section 4: Possible Challenges and Cautions (`challenges_and_cautions`)
- **Purpose**: Highlights planetary friction, potential pitfalls, and areas requiring deliberate prudence.
- **Formulation**: Assesses malefic influences (Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Ketu), 6th/8th/12th house activations, combustion, and transitional Hora boundaries.

### Section 5: Key Time Windows (`key_time_windows`)
- **Purpose**: Pinpoints specific auspicious intervals for high-leverage execution and periods requiring caution.
- **Formulation**: Incorporates active Hora windows, Abhijit Muhurta, Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, and lunar phases.

### Section 6: Relevant Chart Facts (`relevant_chart_facts`)
- **Purpose**: Transparent disclosure of calculated mathematical values.
- **Formulation**: Cites natal Lagna sign/degree, Moon sign/nakshatra, active Vimshottari Mahadasha/Antardasha, and transit alignments.

### Section 7: Applicable Traditional Rules (`applicable_traditional_rules`)
- **Purpose**: Establishes transparent epistemic provenance by citing classical rules.
- **Formulation**: Grounded citations of Parashara, Jaimini, KP, or Tajika principles.

### Section 8: Practical Reflection (`practical_reflection`)
- **Purpose**: Translates astrological insights into constructive personal habits, mindfulness exercises, and practical focus.
- **Formulation**: Actionable advice (e.g. reviewing financial budgets, mindful communication, deep-work periods).

### Section 9: Limitations & Epistemic Scope (`limitations`)
- **Purpose**: Reaffirms ethical boundaries and transparency.
- **Formulation**: Explicitly disclaims fatalism and reinforces that personal initiative (Purushartha), moral discernment, and practical competence govern lived outcomes.

## 3. Horizon-Specific Methodologies (AstroVerse Life Map)

### 3.1 Hourly Horizon (Planetary Horas)
- **Timeframe**: Current solar hour interval (typically 50–70 minutes depending on season and latitude).
- **Planetary Sequence**: Classical Chaldean order: Saturn &rarr; Jupiter &rarr; Mars &rarr; Sun &rarr; Venus &rarr; Mercury &rarr; Moon.
- **Calculation**: Exact topocentric sunrise and sunset times computed using Swiss Ephemeris. Day is divided into 12 equal diurnal Horas; night into 12 nocturnal Horas.

### 3.2 Daily Horizon (Solar-Lunar Gochara)
- **Timeframe**: 24-hour solar day from sunrise to next sunrise.
- **Methodology**: Combines weekday lord, active Tithi, lunar nakshatra, and major daylight divisions (Rahu Kaal, Abhijit Muhurta).

### 3.3 Weekly Horizon (7-Day Lunar Cycle)
- **Timeframe**: Upcoming seven-day period.
- **Methodology**: Traces transiting Moon traversing 2 to 3 zodiac signs (approximately 2.3 days per sign) relative to natal Lagna and Chandra Lagna.

### 3.4 Monthly Horizon (4-Week Gochara Synthesis)
- **Timeframe**: Four-week solar/lunar month.
- **Methodology**: Analyzes fast-moving planet transits (Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars) across natal houses, synthesized with current Mahadasha-Antardasha sub-periods.

### 3.5 Yearly Horizon (Tajika Varshaphal & Annual Transits)
- **Timeframe**: Annual planetary progression and birthday solar return.
- **Methodology**: Evaluates exact Solar Return instant, moving Muntha house, Varsheshwara (Year Lord), and major transits of Jupiter, Saturn, and Rahu/Ketu.

### 3.6 Mahadasha Horizon (Multi-Year Karmic Arc)
- **Timeframe**: Major Vimshottari planetary periods (6 to 20 years per Mahadasha).
- **Methodology**: Evaluates natal house placement of Dasha lord, its relationship with Ascendant, and Antardasha sub-cycles.
