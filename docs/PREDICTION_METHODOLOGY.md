# AstroVerse Prediction Methodology & Parashari Jyotish Framework

## 1. Classical Astrological Foundation

AstroVerse prediction algorithms are derived from classical Vedic texts—chiefly the *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra* (BPHS), *Phaladeepika*, and the *Saravali*—combined with the classical Chaldean planetary order for diurnal and nocturnal Horas.

The platform rejects arbitrary superstition and fatalistic pronouncements. Instead, predictions represent qualitative astrological tendencies and auspicious time windows designed to empower informed, ethical action.

---

## 2. Standard 7-Section Architecture

Every structured prediction generated across any horizon strictly complies with the standardized 7-section framework:

### Section A: Overall Outlook
- **Purpose**: Provides a concise executive synthesis of celestial conditions for the selected timeframe.
- **Formulation**: Synthesizes the active planetary ruler with the natal Lagna and prevailing Dasha cycle.

### Section B: Supportive Developments
- **Purpose**: Identifies favorable endeavors, strategic opportunities, and harmonious alignments.
- **Formulation**: Evaluates benefic aspects (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, waxing Moon), Kendra/Trikona activations, and Dhana/Raja Yoga triggers.

### Section C: Possible Challenges and Cautions
- **Purpose**: Highlights planetary friction, potential pitfalls, and areas requiring deliberate prudence.
- **Formulation**: Assesses malefic influences (Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Ketu), 6th/8th/12th house activations, combustion, and transitional Hora boundaries.

### Section D: Relevant Time Windows
- **Purpose**: Pinpoints specific auspicious intervals for high-leverage execution and periods requiring caution.
- **Formulation**: Incorporates active Hora windows, Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, and lunar phases (Shukla vs Krishna Paksha).

### Section E: Why This Applies (Astrological Grounding)
- **Purpose**: Establishes transparent epistemic provenance by explaining *why* the prediction was generated.
- **Formulation**: Cites natal Lagna sign/degree, Moon sign/nakshatra, active Vimshottari Mahadasha and Antardasha, and relevant planetary relationships.

### Section F: Practical Reflection
- **Purpose**: Translates astrological insights into constructive personal habits, mindfulness exercises, and practical focus.
- **Formulation**: Grounded action items (e.g. reviewing financial budgets, mindful communication, deep-work periods).

### Section G: Limitations & Epistemic Scope
- **Purpose**: Reaffirms ethical boundaries and transparency.
- **Formulation**: Explicitly disclaims fatalism and reinforces that personal initiative (Purushartha), moral discernment, and practical competence govern lived outcomes.

## 3. Horizon-Specific Methodologies

### 3.1 Hourly Horizon (Planetary Horas)
- **Timeframe**: Current solar hour interval (typically 50–70 minutes depending on season and latitude).
- **Planetary Sequence**: Classical Chaldean order: Saturn &rarr; Jupiter &rarr; Mars &rarr; Sun &rarr; Venus &rarr; Mercury &rarr; Moon.
- **Calculation**: Exact sunrise and sunset times computed for user's geographical coordinates using Swiss Ephemeris. Day is divided into 12 equal diurnal Horas; night into 12 nocturnal Horas.
- **Application**: Immediate strategic focus, negotiation timing, focused analytical tasks, and emotional composure.

### 3.2 Weekly Horizon (7-Day Lunar Cycle)
- **Timeframe**: Upcoming seven-day period.
- **Methodology**: Traces transiting Moon traversing 2 to 3 zodiac signs (approximately 2.3 days per sign) relative to natal Lagna and Chandra Lagna.
- **Application**: Mid-range workflow planning, scheduling critical meetings during favorable lunar Kendra transits, avoiding commitments during 8th house lunar transit (Ashtama Shani/Chandra).

### 3.3 Monthly Horizon (4-Week Gochara Synthesis)
- **Timeframe**: Four-week solar/lunar month.
- **Methodology**: Analyzes fast-moving planet transits (Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars) across natal houses, synthesized with current Mahadasha-Antardasha sub-periods.
- **Application**: Monthly budgeting, major career initiatives, domestic harmonizing, and health optimization.

### 3.4 Yearly Horizon (12-Month Strategic Cycle)
- **Timeframe**: Annual planetary progression.
- **Methodology**: Traces slow-moving transits—Jupiter (1 year per sign), Saturn (2.5 years per sign), and Rahu/Ketu (1.5 years per sign)—against natal D1 and D9 charts.
- **Application**: Long-term capital allocation, professional career transitions, educational milestones, and spiritual recalibration.
